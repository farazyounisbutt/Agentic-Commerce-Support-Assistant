# Shipping Policy

Northstar Outfitters processes in-stock orders within two business days, Monday through Friday. Standard delivery usually takes three to five business days after an order ships. Expedited delivery, when offered at checkout, usually takes one to two business days after shipment.

We send a shipment confirmation email with the carrier and tracking number as soon as a package leaves our warehouse. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show its first scan.

Northstar Outfitters currently ships within the continental United States. We do not offer international shipping, freight forwarding, or PO box delivery.

If a package is delayed, please check the tracking link first. Contact support if tracking has not updated for five business days or if the estimated delivery date has passed.
