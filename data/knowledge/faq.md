# Frequently Asked Questions

## Can I change or cancel an order?

Contact support as soon as possible. We can only request a change or cancellation before an order enters shipment processing; we cannot guarantee that a request will be successful.

## How do I find my order status?

Use your order number from the confirmation email. When an order ships, the shipment confirmation includes the carrier and tracking number.

## What if an item is out of stock?

Catalog availability reflects the current variant stock level. Select another available size or color, or check back later; Northstar Outfitters does not offer backorders for this demo store.

## How should I care for outdoor apparel?

Follow the care label on each item. Avoid fabric softener on technical shells and wash waterproof garments with a cleaner designed for technical outerwear.
