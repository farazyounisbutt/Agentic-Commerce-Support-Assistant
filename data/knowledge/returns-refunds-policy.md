# Returns and Refunds Policy

Most unused Northstar Outfitters items may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Products must be clean, unworn, and returned with original tags and packaging where applicable. Final-sale items and gift cards are not eligible for return.

Once the return arrives and is inspected, an eligible refund is issued to the original payment method. Allow five to ten business days for the financial institution to post the refund.

If an order arrives damaged or with the wrong item, contact support within seven days of delivery with your order number and clear photos of the item and packaging. A support specialist will review the situation before any replacement or refund is approved.

We do not offer direct exchanges. Return an eligible item for a refund, then place a new order for the desired size or color while stock is available.
